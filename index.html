VoIP connectivity

Test your setup, safely.

Use this page as a starting point for checking a VoIP installation in an environment you own or are explicitly authorized to test.

This page does not scan networks or place calls. It makes no requests to other hosts. Run connectivity checks through your provider’s approved tools and only against systems you are authorized to assess.

Suggested checklist

  1. Confirm your SIP account and test destination with your VoIP provider.
  2. Use the provider’s documented test procedure on a network you control.
  3. Check firewall and NAT settings against the provider’s official requirements.
  4. Review call quality, registration status, and logs in your approved phone or PBX.
For authorized troubleshooting only. Contact your VoIP administrator or provider for help with account-specific tests.
